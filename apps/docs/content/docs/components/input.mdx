`aria-invalid` is what drives the error styling — there is no `error` prop. That
keeps the visual state and the accessible state from ever disagreeing.
[`Field`](/docs/components/field) sets it for you.

Number inputs have their spinner buttons suppressed globally by
[the theme](/docs/theme#base-rules); they are unusable at NUI scale and invite
mis-clicks over a moving game.

## Surface

`bg-black/[0.05]` in light, `bg-white/10` in dark — translucent rather than a
solid fill, so a field sitting on a card and a field sitting on the page
background both look right without a variant.

Focus draws a 3px `ring-ring/50` and shifts the border. Text is `text-base` on
mobile widths and `text-sm` from `md` up, which is the standard shadcn trick to
stop iOS zooming on focus; it is inert in CEF but harmless.
