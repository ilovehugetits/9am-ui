## The trend is a whole sentence

`trend.label` is the finished, already-translated string. The card decides the
arrow and the colour from the **sign** of `trend.percent`, but it never builds
the text:

```tsx
trend={{
  percent: 12.5,
  label: t("ui.overview.fromLastWeek", { percent: "12.5%" }),
}}
```

Pluralisation, word order and where the sign goes are the locale's business.
A component that assembled `"+" + percent + "% " + t("fromLastWeek")` would be
correct in English and wrong in most other languages.

The magnitude is unused — only the sign matters. Positive takes
`TrendingUpIcon` in primary, negative takes `TrendingDownIcon` in red, and
exactly zero is muted with the up arrow.
